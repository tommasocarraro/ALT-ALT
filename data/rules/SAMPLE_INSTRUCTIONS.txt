SIMPLE PIVOT, ALLOY  (Simple Pivot)

1. Remove: 6900 2RS
   Put these on the Stud in this order, from one end to the other:
     a. Handle (or a Standard Nut): turn this end to tighten
     b. Drift RE 16 Aluminum: pushes the inner ring
     c. Pilot Short 10 Aluminum: inside the bore, on the Stud
     d. [the bearing to remove (6900 2RS), in its seat]
     e. Sleeve 22 Aluminum: against the part, around the seat; the bearing or axle moves into it
     f. Step Aluminum: closes the open end of the sleeve
     g. Stud Stop (or a Standard Nut): holds the other end; it needs a piece larger than 20 mm to sit against
   Use 1 O-ring on the Stud to keep the pilot from sliding.
   Stud length: choose with the part in hand.
   Then: Tighten a nut. The drift pushes on the inner ring and the bearing comes out of its seat into the sleeve. If the tool starts to sit crooked, stop and check.
   Note: A Stud Stop may replace either nut if it sits against a piece larger than 20 mm.
   Official diagram: 2SBP-R.pdf

2. Install: 6900 2RS
   Put these on the Stud in this order, from one end to the other:
     a. Handle (or a Standard Nut): turn this end to tighten
     b. Drift RE 22 Aluminum: flat side on the bearing, unless the manufacturer asks for the relief side
     c. Pilot Short 10 Aluminum: inside the bore, on the Stud
     d. [the new bearing (6900 2RS)]
     e. [the part, with the empty seat towards the bearing]
     f. Drift RE 24 Aluminum: relief side gives 3 mm of room
     g. Stud Stop (or a Standard Nut): holds the other end; it needs a piece larger than 20 mm to sit against
   Use 1 O-ring on the Stud to keep the pilot from sliding.
   Stud length: choose with the part in hand.
   Then: Start the bearing square in its seat, then tighten a nut until the bearing is fully home. It takes little force: if it gets hard, stop and check that the bearing is straight.
   Alternative for the leverage: Step + Sleeve: if the pilot or the inner ring would touch the leverage drift before the bearing is home
   Note: A Stud Stop may replace either nut if it sits against a piece larger than 20 mm.
   Note: Which face of the Drift RE presses follows the manufacturer: the flat side by default, the relief side when the bearing has to be slightly loaded.
   Official diagram: 2SBP-I.pdf

     d. Pilot Short 15 Aluminum: inside the bore, on the Stud
     e. [the bearing to remove (6902 2RS), in its seat]

     g. Stud Stop (or a Standard Nut): holds the other end; it needs a piece larger than 20 mm to sit against
