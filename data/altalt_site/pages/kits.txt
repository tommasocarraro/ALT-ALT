Foundation 
Start with one of the Hub & Suspension Bearing Press Kits as a foundation. Although we’ll end up with both versions of the Sleeve and Drift RE, the aluminum HSBP-2 kit has an aluminum Step, Spacer Tube and Pilots. A good choice for durability.
View fullsize
Second Set of Drift RE
When working on BSB configuration hubs, some manufacturers recommend using a second Drift as the leverage piece when installing both bearings. For the first bearing, the Drift leverages from the opposite side of the part in the empty bearing bore. For the second bearing install, a second drift is used as leverage against the first installed bearing. So we’ll need a second set of Drift RE. And considering that you may also use the Drift RE to leverage the install of suspension bearings, we’ll choose the acetal version of the Drift RE Pack so that you have the option to leverage with a paint and carbon friendly acetal Drift RE.
View fullsize
Second Set of Sleeve
As with the second set of Drift RE above, the Sleeves are used to leverage the removal of bearings. When working on painted or carbon surfaces, a nice soft acetal Sleeve is your best choice, but only if you have a substantial and perpendicular surface to leverage against. 
If the surface is perpendicular but not substantial, you’ll have to use the aluminum Sleeves, but ALWAYS be careful when leveraging small/thin surfaces because your manufacturer probably doesn’t recommend you do this. I DON’T recommend (or warrant) it either, but lots of you seem to want to do it.
If the surface is substantial but not perpendicular then you could try shaping the acetal sleeve to work, but be CAREFUL. This is also NOT recommended practice (because I can’t control it), but lots of people do it. Replacements will be available.
