ALT Extractor
The ALT Extractor removes the first bearing in BSB* and double stacked pivot arrangements. 
When trying to remove the first bearing in a BSB arrangements, the bearing spacer between the bearings impedes your access to the back side of the bearing. In a double stacked arrangement there is no space between the bearings. This lack of access to the back side of the bearing will keep you from using a standard bearing press to remove the first bearing. 
In these arrangements, the ALT Extractor will securely grab this bearing so that you can press it out. And, in the BSB arrangement it will do it in a way that doesn’t damage the bearing spacer that sits behind it. 
View fullsize
Use the ALT Extractor instead of the ALT Drift when:
In BSB when bearing spacer doesn’t move off to the side.
In BSB the bearing spacer is shorter that 10mm long.
In double stacked pivot when the bearings are side by side. 
If you are going to have to deal with these arrangements in the future. The ALT Extractor has less restrictions than the ALT Drift. 
* BSB - Bearing/Spacer/Bearing

The Tool
Basically the ALT Extractor is designed to grab onto a bearing. Once attached, you can use the ALT Extractor’s threaded stud, along with some leveraging pieces, to pull the bearing out of it’s bore. Just like the controlled motion of a bearing press.
View fullsize
How it works
The ALT Extractor design is based around a split collet with a very small lip on it. The tool goes in through the front of the bearing and the Split Collet expands into the bearing inner diameter AND the very small gap between the bearing and bearing spacer. 
Once engaged, the ALT Extractor can pull the bearing out using the leverage pieces of my bearing presses.

Why it works great
The tool expands until it hits the inside of the bearing inner ring ensuring a tight grasp AND a perfect placement of the small lip that hooks the back side of the bearing. The fact that the tool automatically stops in this position ensures that the lip never over expands which is something that could damage the bearing spacer that sits behind the bearing.
View fullsize
The design is further stabilized by the way the wedge piece fits into and expands the split collet. The Wedge has 4 symmetrical angled faces that are always in full contact with the split collet. That means that as you expand the split collet and apply force to remove the bearing, nothing shifts.

Sizes:
BEARING I.D. (inner diameter): 
Extractors come in sizes which correspond to the bearing inner diameter (ID): 
10, 12, 15, 17, 18, 20 & 25mm
BEARING WIDTH:
Sizes 10 & 12 fit widths 5-12mm
Sizes 15 thru 25 come in two width versions:
Regular version fits bearings 5-14mm wide for new system (June 2024)
OG version fits bearings 5-12mm for old system (before June 2024)
IMPORTANT
Identify if you have a new or old system before order ALT Extractors. Identify your system by looking at the Drifts that came with your system.
OLD SYSTEM (before June 2024) - The Sleeves in these kits were shorter than in the new system, and they can only allow the removal of 12mm wide bearings using the ALT Extractors. 
If you have a kit sold BEFORE June 2024, choose “OG” versions of the ALT Extractors. 
If the Drifts in your kit look like this BOTH SIDES, then you have the old system and need “OG” ALT Extractors.
NEW SYSTEM (June 2024) - Longer Sleeves allow for the removal of wider bearings. 
If you have a kit, or are buying a new kit (with Sleeves), choose ALT Extractors WITHOUT “OG” in the product name. These are regular versions.
If the Drifts in your kit have a relief on ONE SIDE, then you have the new system and can use regular ALT Extractors.
Features:
Designed to have a strong grasp on the bearing being removed.
Designed to not damage the bearing spacer behind the bearing being removed.
Secure grasp of bearing because collet expands inside bearing to contact the bearing ID.
Solid, full contact expansion of split collet thanks to the wedge design.
Does not require the bearing spacer to move off to the side.
Works with a controllable screwing motion. No hammering or yanking. 
Split collet (part that grabs the bearing) is made of durable stainless steel.
