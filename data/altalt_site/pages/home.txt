How To Use The Website
Are you new to bearing swaps, bearing presses or just new to my system?
New to bearing swaps and presses?
Start with the INSTRUCTION topics. On the Bearing Basics page you’ll learn about bearings.  On the How-To page you’ll learn about how bearings should get removed and installed and the PLCC (Press, Leverage, Center & Clearance) method. This is a method to follow when removing and installing EVERY bearing. The Instructional Diagrams page will show you how to set the tool up for some common bearing arrangements.
Then…
 New to my system? 
Here you already know about bearing basics and how to use a press, so just head over to my System Explained page to find out how it works and what it works on. It’s kind of a Lego modular design approach, so if you have any questions, please use the Contact to email me. 
ALSO, the Pieces page explains every piece in the system.
